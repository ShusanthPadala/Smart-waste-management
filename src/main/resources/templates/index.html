          How it is calculated

          Forecast

          A least-squares trend line over each bin's last 10 hourly readings (restarted after a collection) gives the fill rate and the hour it reaches 100%. R² becomes the confidence score.

          Priority

          Overflow risk: full or past 100% on trend. Urgent: ≥ 90% or full within 8 h. Soon: full within 24 h.

          Routing

          Bins are split between trucks with a capacity-aware sweep around the depot. Each tour starts with nearest-neighbour and is improved with 2-opt. Distances use the Haversine formula.

          Emissions

          Fuel = distance ÷ truck km/L (4.5 by default). CO₂ = fuel × 2.68 kg/L diesel. One tree absorbs ≈ 21 kg CO₂ a year.
